## Analysis (written after the runs, from the numbers above and the per-run reports)

**At low steady load (2 x 10 traces/s) the pipeline meets the budget.**
- End to end (root end to `trace.completed`): p95 37.2 s, within the measured part of the budget (40.1 s) and the 60 s SLO.
- The tail_sampling wait is 99 % of root end to ClickHouse.
- The insert stage is 0.18 s p50 and 0.27 s p95.
- No stage is over its budget.

**Under load, one stage is over budget: enqueue -> ClickHouse.** It covers the exporter's sending queue, the INSERT, the async-insert buffer and the materialized view.
- At 2 x 50 traces/s, steady, it reaches p95 39.9 s (49 % of the lag), and end to end is 73.6 s at p95.
- Under the stress profile, 2 x 100 traces/s with the 2 -> 4 -> 2 scaling, it reaches p95 215.3 s (81 %), and end to end is 242.1 s at p95.

The other stages do not change with load: gateway -> decided stays at 30.5 s p95, and the agent hops stay under 60 ms.

**It is a queue that builds up, not a fixed offset.**
- The gateways' exporter queue (`otelcol_exporter_queue_size{exporter="clickhouse"}`, in requests; a request is one released trace) grows during the load. It peaks at 21 at low load, 2,777 at 100/s and 20,710 under stress, and drains after the load ends.
- Stage 5's p95 rises from bucket to bucket at 100/s and under stress (per-run reports, "Stage p95 by root end time").

**Throughput, measured:**

| run | gateways | kept traces offered (exported spans / 4 / 360 s) | exporter queue | ClickHouse pending async inserts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| low | 2 | ~16/s | <= 21 | 5-20 |
| steady100 | 2 | ~81/s | grows to 2,777 (about 8 traces/s short) | 20-22 |
| stress, T+30..120 s | 2 | ~162/s | grows | 20 |
| stress, T+150..240 s | 4 | ~162/s | about flat; stage-5 p95 stays at ~116 s | 40-44 |
| stress, T+270..360 s | 2 | ~162/s | grows again | 20 |

What the numbers show:
- **One in-flight insert per consumer.** Whenever the queue builds, ClickHouse's `PendingAsyncInsert` sits at 20 with 2 gateways and 40 with 4. That is gateways x the exporter's 10 consumers (the default `num_consumers`; at 0.164 the ClickHouse exporter's queue has no batching unless it is configured).
- **About 4 inserts per second per consumer.** tail_sampling 0.164.1 releases each sampled trace as its own `ConsumeTraces` call, so each insert carries one trace. At 100/s the 20 consumers completed about 73 inserts/s (81 offered minus the queue's growth). The raw insert counter is no help here: ClickHouse's `InsertQuery` also counts the assembler's `trace_summary` inserts.
- **Reading, not proof.** With `wait_for_async_insert=1` and the adaptive busy timeout of 50-200 ms (ClickHouse 26.8 settings on this server), each consumer would wait for the buffer flush before its next insert. That fits the numbers, but this bench cannot separate the queue wait from the insert itself. No stamp is possible after the exporter's queue, and `query_log` / `asynchronous_insert_log` are not enabled on this server. The follow-up issue measures it before anything is changed.

**Capacity of the current configuration.** The highest tested load within budget is 20 traces/s in total. At 100 traces/s, steady with 2 gateways, the queue builds and end to end is over the SLO. So with this trace mix (4 spans, 50 % error traces) the configuration sustains the SLO at **20 traces/s tested, and less than 100 traces/s**. The limit lies at or below about 73 kept traces/s for 2 gateways, the rate their exporters achieved at 100/s.

**Stage 1 is a pipeline measurement in every run.** The load generator's own backlog (span end to the start of its export call) stayed under 60 ms at p95 in every run, so no profile is marked "load generator saturated".
- The 2026-11-04 report ran the same 2 x 100/s stress profile with the same send loop, but did not record the backlog. R3 reproduces its numbers: root end to ClickHouse p95 235 s here, 231 s there, with the generator keeping up. So the 2026-11-04 lag was very likely pipeline lag too, not generator backlog. This is an inference.

**The stamps do not distort what they measure.** At the low profile, the run with stamps differs from the run without by -0.01 / +0.00 s (p50 / p95) to the first ClickHouse arrival, and by -0.17 / -0.20 s end to end. Both are within the tolerance in `stage-budget.env`.

**Notes on the metrics:**
- `otelcol_processor_tail_sampling_sampling_traces_on_memory` is not a backlog of undecided traces here. gateway.yaml configures no `decision_cache`, so 0.164.1 uses a no-op cache. A decided trace then stays in the processor's map until the `num_traces` ring (200,000) evicts it, and the gauge counts every trace seen since the pod started (7,200 at low load, 61,566 under stress). This is reported, not judged; it is not over any budget here.
- With the stamps overlay, `traceblame_normalizer_unmapped_attrs_total` also counts `traceblame.bench.t_*`.
- Split traces (1,206 under stress) take two paths and are excluded from the per-stage table. The rejoin counts are in the stress report's assembler section.
